## Valixara

**AI that acts — and proves what it did.**
*AI hành động — và chứng minh được đã làm gì.*

Businesses are ready to put AI to work in real operations, not demos. The hard part is not capability — it is
trust: when AI acts, who can prove what it did, on which data, and why?

We help traditional and operational businesses bring AI into their workflows so they can scale faster and more
sustainably, with every material AI action recorded, verifiable and explainable.

### What we stand for

- **Verified by default** — action history you can audit, not slideware.
- **Control without freezing speed** — a fast path when needed, full provenance when stakes are high.
- **Governance by construction** — built in from day one, not bolted on after launch.
- **Dogfood first** — we run our own work on the systems we build.

### How we work

Evidence over claims. We do not announce certifications we do not hold, and we do not invent customer logos.
Progress is measured by what runs, what is measured, and what can be re-checked.
